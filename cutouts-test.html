Artist statement

American science fiction movies, television shows, cartoons and superhero comic book escapades were regular imports growing up in the Philippines. Thanks to action figure toys, the stories continued well beyond the screen and comic pages. The Cutout Figures Series is a set of photographs of paper cut-outs that revisit those toys and boyhood narratives, while addressing cultural hegemonies and American hero tropes.
