Artist statement

Posed Encounters Series

I am interested in particular cinematic gestures in movies. From Spielberg reels to Godzilla camp, there's a certain reaction shot that is reiterated over and over, employed perhaps to mirror, augment and tap into collective anxieties. I use my own brand of camp and noir-like effect to explore this idea of anxiety and of the sublime.
