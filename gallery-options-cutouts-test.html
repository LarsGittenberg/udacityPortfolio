Cutout Figures Series

American science fiction movies, television shows, cartoons and superhero comic book escapades were regular imports growing up in the Philippines. Thanks to action figure toys, the stories continued well beyond the screen and comic pages. These photographs of paper cut-outs revisit those toys and boyhood narratives, while addressing cultural hegemonies and American hero tropes.
